Teaching Interests

Introduction to Financial Accounting Research

Graduate Level

This course introduces graduate students to the foundations and development of financial accounting research, from early normative approaches to modern empirical research. Topics include the information role of accounting, capital market research, information processing, and emerging research areas such as social media and the public sector.

Big Data and Business Analytics

Undergraduate Level

This course introduces students to the fundamental concepts and methods of big data and business analytics. Topics include data preprocessing, data mining, regression, classification, clustering, and other analytical techniques for extracting useful insights from large and complex data to support business decision-making.

Introduction to Financial Accounting

Undergraduate Level

This course introduces students to the fundamental concepts and principles of financial accounting and the role of accounting information in business decision-making. Topics include financial reporting, accounting standards, and the preparation and interpretation of the major financial statements.
